Revenue shown throughout (Daily and MTD) is Room Revenue plus each property's Sales-Centers non-room lines (Lidos carries a small Misc line, £281.71 MTD; Victoria £77.51 MTD; both included in the Revenue totals shown here).

Occupancy % = 1,006 rooms sold ÷ (1,006 + 412 available) = 70.9%. Today's OOO (2) = 49 total rooms − 11 sold − 36 available, within the typical 0–3 range.
